## 5. Art direction — "polished shapes" (v23, finished in phase 8)
**Goal:** the same shape-only world, crafted so it reads as a deliberate vector-illustration style rather than primitives with outlines: bold silhouettes, two-to-three-tone shading from one light, a dark rim, soft contact shadows, glints, glow on anything magical, life in every idle. Original look — do not imitate a particular game.
1. **One light.** Direction top-left (≈ −135°): highlights top-left, shade bottom-right, cast shadows offset down-right. A **light pass** per room: ambient colour per land (Void near-black, Vault warm gold, Orchard shifting with its clock…) at low resolution (¼ scale offscreen, smooth-scaled up), point lights from torches/braziers/fire/lava/spells/the spotlight/lanterns drawn with radial gradients, composited with multiply/screen. Keep the existing dark-room fog as a special case of it.
2. **Shape finish** (applies automatically wherever `shape()` is used): gradient fill from the entity colour (lighter top, darker bottom), 2px rim in `darker(color, .55)`, inner highlight arc, specular dot, contact shadow, **eyes** (two dots/slits positioned per shape, looking toward the player, blinking) so foes feel alive; the white hit flash and the status overlays (frost, burn, stun, poison) stay clearly readable.
3. **Baked sprites.** Anything with gradients/shadows is drawn once per (type, size, colour, state) into an offscreen canvas (`bake(key, w, h, draw)` with an LRU cap) and then `drawImage`d with rotation / squash-and-stretch per frame. Invalidate on theme change only. Never build gradients per frame in hot loops (cache by key).
4. **Animation without frames:** breathing (scale ±3% over ~0.8 s), walk bob and squash tied to velocity, anticipation before attacks (lean back + grow), recoil on hit, death = shards + puff + fading shadow, spawn-in = grow from 0 with a ring. Boss parts get secondary motion (swaying limbs, hovering, charging glows).
5. **World.** A tile painter per land driven by `theme` plus a pattern tag (flagstone, plank, sand ripples, mossy brick, ice cracks, scorched slab, glass, felt/velvet, tile checker…): 3-tone walls with a lit top edge and a shaded face, ambient occlusion along wall bases, scattered rubble/decor, props with shading and cast shadows, doors/portals/gates as crafted objects (stone frame, glyph, glow), animated liquids (caustics, bubbling lava) — all static parts baked into `room.cache`. 1–2 cheap **ambient particle systems per land** (dust motes, embers, snow, spores, bubbles, petals, stars, fireflies).
6. **FX & telegraphs — one visual language.** Telegraph = a zone filled in the damage colour at low alpha + dashed/hatched interior + a bright rim that closes in as time runs out; projectiles = shaded core + glow + short trail (additive blending, capped); hits = white flash + 6–10 sparks; crits = star burst; heals = rising motes; pickups bob and sparkle by rarity. Screen-space effects (damage vignette, low-HP pulse, boss-intro letterbox, flashes) obey `settings.flash` / `shake`.
7. **UI chrome.** One panel style (dark glass, 2px border, subtle inner gradient, corner ornaments drawn as paths), button states (idle/hover/pressed/disabled/selected), a redrawn icon set (same names in `ED.icons`), HUD with gloss on hearts and bars, an ornate boss-bar frame, tooltip cards, parchment-style intro cards. Every existing screen (Versions, Guide, ROUTES, Collection, Hearth, Hunt, Legacy…) adopts it. **Fonts:** keep the monospace stack for body text (the tests measure it); a display stack is allowed for large headings only, with overflow checked.
8. **Readability beats beauty.** Priority: player + incoming damage > foes > pickups/interactables > decor. Decor stays calmer than gameplay objects. Hidden things (cracks, portals, trapdoor seams) get a *subtle but findable* tell (a glint every ~3 s). Never red-vs-green only: pair colours with shape/pattern cues.
9. **Contrast test.** Telegraph/hazard/projectile colours keep ≥ 3:1 luminance contrast against each land's `floorA/floorB` (test it from the registry data for every land, old and new).
10. **Performance.** Measured in the pane (snippet in HANDOFF/previous sessions: loop `g.step(); g.render()` and time them): typical render ≤ 8 ms average, heaviest gallery scene ≤ 14 ms, no `shadowBlur` in loops, offscreen canvases reused. Effects ladder `settings.effects` 0/1/2; auto-degrade when the rolling average > 15 ms; `prefers-reduced-motion` starts at 1. Effects "Off" must still look better than today (outline + shading) but cost nothing extra.
11. **Determinism.** Cosmetic randomness comes from a cosmetic PRNG, never `Math.random`/`g.rng`; rendering mutates nothing. Test: a scripted run with and without `g.render()` ends in the same state hash.
12. **Classic stays reachable.** With v23 off the classic renderer's draw calls are unchanged.
**Minimum bar for "done"** (the long tail may rely on the generic finish): the player and all 11 classes, the 25 most-met foes, every MEGA boss, every hazard/telegraph, every door/gate/portal/hidden tell, all 25 lands' floors, and every UI screen are individually reviewed in screenshots and look intentional; every other foe and boss passes the silhouette/readability review in the contact sheets.

## 7. Quality bar for every foe, boss, hazard and land
* A behaviour the game does not already have, a one-line description (bestiary), a distinct shape + colour + silhouette, a telegraph before any damage, no unavoidable damage, nothing that makes a foe LEAVE without dying while it carries a key (`e.carriesKey`), nothing spawned inside a brazier/prop, hazards respect the screen-flash setting, no permanent loss of items.
* Tests per entity: spawn → behaviour assertion → finite state → dies/clears; **mutation-verify** each new behaviour test (break the behaviour in an `iso.sh` copy, see the test fail). Bosses: the P20 pattern. Lands: rule applies only in that land, off when the pack is off, never changes the plain stairs.
* When a battery stalls, investigate with the key-solver text (`ED.keys.describeSources`) before blaming the bot; real bugs found so far were all of the "something leaves / is lost / is unreachable" family.

## 9. Pitfalls we already paid for (details in HANDOFF "Gotchas")
* `ED.runHooks` forwards only TWO extra args; never `P.track('relics', …)` in a pack; a hook that throws is disabled after 3 errors in the browser (tests keep it strict).
* `hold` freezes a foe's WHOLE update including boss scripts; `hitstop` freezes the world after `hurtPlayer`/`destroyPart` (step ≥ 40 frames in tests); lowering a phased boss's hp below an `at` threshold replaces your scripted attack; `p.guard` swallows the first test hit; a modal (milestone, talent, **sacrifice**) freezes the world — whole-floor test loops must let the bot answer sacrifice modals (only `route_cost` may be cleared by the loop).
* Anything that makes a foe leave without dying must skip key carriers; foes spawned inside a brazier cannot be hit by shots; lands' foes join pools through `ED.freeFoes`/`W.add`'s `enemies` — check `eligible()` for routed lands (their introduction moves with the land).
* Tests share `ED.save.meta` and `ED.packs` override within one process: restore what you change (P0i does). New sections that play frames go after the layout-sensitive ones. **Anything that calls `Math.random` during rendering shifts the sandbox's seeded stream and changes later tests — keep art RNG-free.**
* `ED.worlds.use(run)` must be called after editing `run.route`/`run.mid` in a test; the last started run stays bound to the resolver.
* Boss intro tips and land rules are wrapped by `UI.tipLines` / the intro hook: check long names and rules in the pane.
* The browser pane's screenshots can lag one frame behind a `render()` call made from JS: take two.
